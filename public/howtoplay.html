HOW TO PLAY

1

JOIN OR CREATE

Pick a nickname and enter the battlefield. You can join a Quick Match to find random opponents or Create a Room to invite your friends with a 4-digit code.

2

PREPARE FOR CHAOS

Matches start with at least 2 players. Once the host clicks start, you'll have a 3-second countdown to focus. Hold your breath.

3

COMPLETE THE TASKS

Each round throws a new mechanical challenge at you. Clicking shapes, typing words, solving math, or finding numbers. Do it FAST.

4

DON'T BE LAST

The timer bar at the top shows how much time is left. Even if you finish, you aren't safe. The slowest player (the Loser) is eliminated every single round.

5

LAST ONE STANDING

Rounds continue until only one survivor remains. That legend takes the crown while the losers suffer the toxic ragebait messages.
