PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how LAST IS THE LOSER ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal identifying information (PII) like your real name, email, or address. We only collect:

  • Nicknames: Temporary names you choose for the game lobby.
  • Game Data: Reaction times and scores within the game session.
  • Cookies & Identifiers: We use cookies and similar technologies for Google AdSense to serve personalized ads based on your interests.

2. How We Use Information

The information is used to:

  • Maintain game sessions and multiplayer rooms.
  • Display leaderboards during a match.
  • Show relevant advertisements via Google AdSense.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdSense to serve ads. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your temporary game data.

5. Terms of Service

By using this game, you agree not to use offensive nicknames or engage in behavior that disrupts the experience for other players. We reserve the right to remove any player from our lobbies.
